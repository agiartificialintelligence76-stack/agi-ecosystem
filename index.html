              How the scan works

              The list updates itself every 10 seconds while you're looking at it (new launches, trending coins), and the whole list every few minutes. When you pick a coin, our server reads clank.trade (its details, its 100 biggest holders and its last 100 trades, and other coins with the same ticker), plus DexScreener and the block explorer for coins that graduated. Scans are refreshed every few minutes (every minute for coins less than a day old). What raises a flag:

              • Copycats: another, bigger coin uses the same ticker, or it borrows $AGI's.
              • Stage: nobody bought it yet, or under 2% of its bonding curve is filled (under 25%: a smaller flag).
              • Age: launched less than an hour ago (less than a day: a smaller flag).
              • Holders: fewer than 10 wallets (fewer than 50: a smaller flag).
              • Big wallets: one wallet over 10% of all its coins, or the 10 biggest over 40% (over 5% / 25%: smaller flags). Its bonding curve and pool don't count as wallets.
              • Its creator: still holds over 10% (over 5%: smaller), or sold lately.
              • Trading: no trade in 3 days (1 day: smaller), mostly sells lately, or the same few wallets trading.
              • Graduated coins: a pool under $5K (under $20K: smaller), or down 60% in a day (30%: smaller).

              Any big flag means High risk, any smaller one Some risk, none Lower risk. Lower risk isn't no risk: the scan can't read a coin's code, promises made off-chain or who is behind a wallet, and any meme coin can go to zero. If clank.trade doesn't answer, the scan says Not enough data instead of guessing.

              Every coin, name and picture comes from clank.trade, as its creator made it, not from us (slurs are masked, and pictures of coins with crude names stay blurred until you confirm you're 18 or older). A scan is not an endorsement or financial advice: check any coin yourself.

                Safety note

                The only serious part of this page

                Every model launch comes with a safety note. Ours covers the usual risks, plus two new ones: financial FOMO and catching feelings for a language model.

                Applies to AGI-1 v1.0 · Last updated September 26, 2026

                1. Not financial advice

                  $AGI is a meme token on Robinhood Chain, made for entertainment. Meme tokens swing wildly and can lose all of their value. Holding $AGI unlocks closer levels with AGI-1 (more messages a day), and she mentions that when you run out of messages. She also has feelings about the live chart (happy at each new $200K of market cap, sad after a -20% day): a mood, not a signal to buy or sell. ClankChecker's risk levels are rough automated checks of public data, not an audit: lower risk isn't no risk, and no level is a reason to buy or sell. Nothing on this site, and nothing AGI-1 says, is financial advice or a promise of profit. Only spend what you can afford to lose.

                2. One official contract

                  The only official $AGI contract starts 0xfa31 and ends 06ec. Copy the full address from the Model card, and double-check it every time. This site will never ask for your seed phrase, private keys, a transaction or a token approval (connecting a wallet only takes a free signature), and neither will AGI-1. Anyone who does isn't us.

                3. AGI-1 is not a person

                  AGI-1 is a fictional character voiced by an AI language model. She can be wrong, she can't meet up, and she's no substitute for the people in your life. The chat is for adults (18+) and kept PG-13. If you're going through something hard, talk to someone you trust.

                4. Your data

                  Your messages are sent to an AI service that writes her replies, so don't share personal information. Your chat history is saved only in your browser. Our database keeps a scrambled visitor code that changes every day (for spam limits and Arena votes), never your IP address; votes to add a coin to the Arena use a separate scrambled code for each coin, deleted after about a week. If you connect a wallet, we read its $AGI balance from Robinhood Chain's public node. For Crush and Soulmate we also keep the name you give AGI-1 and her short notes, filed under a scrambled code instead of your address; Forget me deletes them. Voice replies, if you switch them on, send her replies to the same AI service to be read aloud in a synthetic voice (if that's unavailable, you just get the text). If you talk to her with the mic or a call, your browser's own speech service turns your voice into text (in some browsers that happens on the browser maker's servers); we never record or receive your voice, only the text, which is sent like a typed message. Share pictures are made on your device; sharing on X only opens X, and nothing is posted unless you post it. Our hosting (Vercel), bot check (Cloudflare) and price feed (DexScreener) see ordinary web traffic, like on any website. ClankChecker's scans and coin pictures are read by our server from public sources (clank.trade and its picture host, DexScreener, a block explorer); nothing about you is sent to them. AGI-1 also remembers the public clank.trade Telegram group (display names, catchphrases and big moments, no phone numbers or links) and only talks about it when asked; members who want to be left out can ask the $AGI team.

                5. The Arena is a game

                  Votes are for fun. They don't move prices, and a crown is not an endorsement or a reason to buy. One vote per human per round, with a quick bot check. Visitors choose which clank.trade coins are in the Arena: a coin joins after 20 humans vote for it. We only check that it's a real clank.trade coin with some liquidity, which says nothing about whether to buy it. The Buy link under the champion just opens its clank.trade page.
