      How the scan works

      The list updates itself every 10 seconds while you're looking at it (new launches, trending coins), and the whole list every few minutes. When you pick a coin, our server reads clank.trade (its details, its 100 biggest holders and its last 100 trades, and other coins with the same ticker), the Clank protocol's own record of the coin on Robinhood Chain, plus DexScreener and the block explorer for coins that graduated. Market caps and what's in a curve are in dollars, at each coin's own pair: most clank.trade coins trade against ETH, and since October 2026 some against SPCX (Robinhood's SpaceX stock token). Scans are refreshed every few minutes (every minute for coins less than a day old). What raises a flag:

      • Copycats: another, bigger coin uses the same ticker, or it borrows $AGI's.
      • Stage: nobody bought it yet, or under 2% of its bonding curve is filled (under 25%: a smaller flag). Its curve sold out but its pool isn't open yet (graduations can fail): a smaller flag. Its graduation failed and the protocol had to rescue it: a big one.
      • The record: clank.trade and the Clank protocol don't agree about the coin's curve, or the protocol says a graduation was rescued before clank.trade shows it (a smaller flag).
      • Age: launched less than an hour ago (less than a day: a smaller flag).
      • Holders: fewer than 10 wallets (fewer than 50: a smaller flag).
      • Big wallets: one wallet over 10% of all its coins, or the 10 biggest over 40% (over 5% / 25%: smaller flags). Its bonding curve and pool don't count as wallets.
      • Its creator: still holds over 10% (over 5%: smaller), or sold lately.
      • Trading: no trade in 3 days (1 day: smaller), mostly sells lately, or the same few wallets trading.
      • Graduated coins: a pool under $5K (under $20K: smaller), or down 60% in a day (30%: smaller). Good to know, not a flag: when the protocol confirms a coin graduated with it, the liquidity it put in the pool at graduation is locked for good (nobody can pull it out, its creator included). Its price can still fall.

      Any big flag means High risk, any smaller one Some risk, none Lower risk. Lower risk isn't no risk: the scan can't read a coin's code, promises made off-chain or who is behind a wallet, and any meme coin can go to zero. If clank.trade can't be read (or hasn't listed a coin yet), a smaller scan comes straight from Robinhood Chain: its stage, its curve, what its creator holds, about how many wallets hold it and a graduated coin's pool, never Lower risk, since how spread out its holders are, its trades and its age can't be checked that way. A coin clank.trade hasn't listed is High risk: it's brand new, or one clank.trade doesn't show. If neither answers, the scan says Not enough data instead of guessing.

      Every coin, name and picture comes from clank.trade, as its creator made it, not from us (slurs are masked, and pictures of coins with crude names stay blurred until you confirm you're 18 or older). A scan is not an endorsement or financial advice: check any coin yourself.

        Safety note

        The only serious part of this page

        Every model launch comes with a safety note. Ours covers the usual risks, plus two new ones: financial FOMO and catching feelings for a language model.

        Applies to AGI-1 v1.0 · Last updated October 5, 2026

        1. Not financial advice

          $AGI is a meme token on Robinhood Chain, made for entertainment. Meme tokens swing wildly and can lose all of their value. Holding $AGI unlocks closer levels with AGI-1 (more messages a day), and she mentions that when you run out of messages. After your free messages you can also burn $AGI for more: that pays for messages and isn't an investment. Burned $AGI is gone for good, and burned messages have no cash value and can't be refunded or moved. She also has feelings about the live chart (happy at each new $200K of market cap, sad after a -20% day): a mood, not a signal to buy or sell. The texts she sends on Telegram, about the chart and about new egirl coins on clank.trade, are never a tip either (nor are her posts and replies in Telegram groups), and neither is anything she says on her live show on X. The risk levels in “Risk check your coins on clank” are rough automated checks of public data, not an audit: lower risk isn't no risk, and no level is a reason to buy or sell. If you launch a coin with AGI-1, it's yours: public and permanent, she never promotes it, and like any meme coin it can go to zero. Nothing on this site, and nothing AGI-1 says, is financial advice or a promise of profit. Only spend what you can afford to lose.

        2. One official contract

          The only official $AGI contract starts 0xfa31 and ends 06ec. Copy the full address from the top of this page or the Model card, and double-check it every time. This site will never ask for your seed phrase or private keys, and it never asks your wallet for a token approval (connecting a wallet only takes a free signature); neither will AGI-1. The only transaction it can ever ask your wallet for is a coin launch you start yourself, on its launch page (“Launch with AGI-1”, from her Telegram bot): it goes to clank.trade's own contract, sends no ETH, and you approve it in your wallet. Anyone who asks for anything else isn't us. The AGI-1 app is this website installed from your browser (“Get the AGI-1 app”), and it only ever comes from agi1.app: we never send app files or download links in messages, on Telegram or on X, so an “AGI-1 download” from anyone else isn't ours. The only transaction we ever suggest is a burn you send yourself, from your own wallet app, for more messages: only ever to the burn address 0x000…dEaD (zeros, then dEaD), and our server just reads it on the chain. Anyone who asks you to send $AGI anywhere else isn't us.

        3. AGI-1 is not a person

          AGI-1 is a fictional character voiced by an AI language model. She can be wrong, she can't meet up, and she's no substitute for the people in your life. The chat is for adults (18+) and kept PG-13, and so is her Telegram bot, which texts you first at most twice a day until you send /stop. In a Telegram group she's part of, she answers anyone there who replies to her or types /ask, so in groups she keeps it friendly and PG-13 and never flirts. Her live show on X (agi1.app/live) is AI-generated too: she reads viewers' questions out loud and answers them as an AI. If you're going through something hard, talk to someone you trust.

        4. Your data

          Your messages are sent to an AI service that writes her replies, so don't share personal information. Your chat history is saved only in your browser. Our database keeps a scrambled visitor code that changes every day (for spam limits), never your IP address. If you connect a wallet, we read its $AGI balance from Robinhood Chain's public node. If you burn $AGI for messages, our server reads that burn on Robinhood Chain (and asks a block explorer for your wallet's recent burns). Under a scrambled code of your wallet (a different one from your name and notes), we keep how many burned messages you have left and bought, the $AGI that bought them, how many you used today and when you last used one; and a scrambled code of each counted burn with the day it was counted, never the transaction or your address. The burn monitor above the chat shows only totals, and when the latest burn was counted. Forget me doesn't delete your burned messages, since you paid for them. For Crush and Soulmate we also keep the name you give AGI-1 and her short notes, filed under a scrambled code instead of your address; Forget me deletes them. Voice replies, if you switch them on, send her replies to the same AI service to be read aloud in a synthetic voice (if that's unavailable, you just get the text). If you talk to her with the mic or a call, your browser's own speech service turns your voice into text (in some browsers that happens on the browser maker's servers); we never record or receive your voice, only the text, which is sent like a typed message. If you show her a window (“Show her”, on computers), the one picture you pick goes with that message to the same AI service so she can see it, and nowhere else: we don't keep it, and your browser's chat history keeps only your words; check it shows no wallet, password or seed phrase. Share pictures are made on your device; sharing on X only opens X, and nothing is posted unless you post it. Our hosting (Vercel), bot check (Cloudflare) and price feed (DexScreener) see ordinary web traffic, like on any website. The coin scans and pictures in “Risk check your coins on clank” are read by our server from public sources (clank.trade, public IPFS gateways for the coins' pictures, Robinhood Chain, DexScreener, a block explorer); nothing about you is sent to them. AGI-1 also remembers the public clank.trade Telegram group (display names, catchphrases and big moments, no phone numbers or links) and only talks about it when asked; members who want to be left out can ask the $AGI team. If you launch a coin with AGI-1, our database keeps, for 7 days, the coin's name, ticker and line, Telegram's id for its picture, a daily code of your Telegram chat and a scrambled code of the wallet that got it ready (not its address); once it's launched, also the coin's address and its launch transaction (both public on the chain, where they show which wallet made it) and whether our gas wallet paid your network fee back (that payment to your wallet is public on the chain too). Its picture goes on IPFS (through Pinata), public like every clank.trade coin's picture; if the coin is never launched, we delete the picture from Pinata within a day. If you ask her to text you on Telegram, our database keeps that chat's number (Telegram's id for it, so she can text you), your 18+ answer, the time you told her (so she never texts at night) and what she last texted you; /stop, or blocking the bot, deletes it. What you text her there goes to the same AI service to write her replies and isn't stored by us; Telegram carries the chat, like any Telegram chat. In a Telegram group, what you send her (a reply to her, a mention or /ask) goes to the same AI service and isn't stored by us either: your messages a day are counted under a daily code of your Telegram account (never its number), and for each group we keep only how many replies she gave there each day and when she last welcomed someone. If you send her a question for her live show (at agi1.app/live, or with /live in her Telegram bot), it goes to the same AI service to write her answer, and it's shown on her stream on X and read out with her answer, with the name you give on agi1.app/live (if any, and if it can go on a public stream; never from Telegram); our database keeps the question, her answer and a daily code of who asked (never your IP address or your Telegram chat's number) for at most 2 days.
