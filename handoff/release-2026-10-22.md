## Additional fixes requested during release

The owner reported incorrect SOL labels on other chains, missing Clank coins, a contract-address search with no
result, and apparently stationary live prices. The exact search case is
`0xFa31d04462746aAA24bC7E5626403E09E4B106ec` on Robinhood Chain.

- HyperEVM's native quote was missing because its returned pool placed WHYPE on the quote side. The parser now
  calculates quote USD from base USD / native price, validates the address, chain and price, and selects the most
  liquid valid pool. A real feed probe returned a valid HYPE quote. The canonical wrapped address is documented at
  https://hyperliquid.gitbook.io/hyperliquid-docs/for-developers/hyperevm/wrapped-hype.
- Missing native quotes now produce an explicit waiting state instead of silently starting a live run in SOL.
  Daily markets remain simulated and now say so alongside their currency. Native results show the native price.
- The relay now discovers trending Clank coins outside its recent window, accepts full contract searches on the
  selected chain, and keeps older selected coins refreshed. Non-Clank contracts can resolve through supported
  DexScreener markets. This does not preload every historical coin or promise coverage absent from upstream sources.
- The client sorts by actual launch time before limiting the New column. Trading statistics survive price-only
  updates and are included in initial snapshots and search responses. Search distinguishes a missing market from
  a temporarily unavailable source; requests, concurrency and caches are bounded.
- Local real-feed browser checks found the exact mixed-case AGI address as Artificial Girlfriend Intelligence,
  with market cap, liquidity, volume and changing candles. The New column started with a one-hour-old launch.
  HyperEVM setup and the running balance used HYPE. A real chart exposed differing provider quotes: graduated coins
  now use the fresh pool quote, fall back to Clank after 60 seconds without one, and return to the pool on recovery.
  Current quotes are never backdated to an older coin's launch when no launch-price history was supplied.
- The final complete production build passed: 1,043 tests passed, zero failed, six skipped, assets and secret scan passed.
  The initial sandboxed attempt could not access integration-test sockets/files; the permitted complete run passed.
  This includes the final timestamp regression and rebuilt assets. Hosted acceptance follows publication;
  do not treat these additional fixes as deployed until verified.

The earlier validation matrix is in `validation-2026-10-22.md`. No real trades, wallet transactions, or social
