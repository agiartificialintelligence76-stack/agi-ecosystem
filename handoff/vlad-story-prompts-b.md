Style: playful hand-inked 2D television cartoon story panel, simple irregular black contour lines, flat cel-shaded characters with restrained two-tone shadows, sparse watercolor pencil background, warm ivory paper texture and soft unfinished ivory vignette edges. Expressive faces and gesture, loose economical scenery. Amber, pale ochre, dusty orange with purple outfit contrast. Light and readable, no dramatic glossy rendering.

No copied reference poses, no pasted full-body portraits, no photorealism, no 3D, no highly detailed concept art.
