Attention is what keeps AGI moving. A flywheel is a heavy wheel: hard to start, then every push keeps it spinning, a little faster each time. Here's ours, in four turns.

  1. 1

    People find AGI-1

    Her live shows on X, her posts and her Telegram groups, and every link someone shares.

  2. 2

    They make and play

    Talk to her, make an AI influencer in AGI Studio, play AGI games: something to do the moment they arrive.

    They share it

    Photos, videos and memes from AGI Studio download with its “Made with AGI Studio” mark, so a post says where it was made.

  4. 4

    More people find AGI-1

    Every share is a new way in, and the wheel turns again, a little faster each time.
