    How it works

    1. 1 $AGI = 1 vote. No cap per wallet: a cap is undone by splitting $AGI over more wallets, so instead each choice also shows how many wallets picked it.
    2. Checked twice. Your $AGI is read when you vote and again when the vote closes, and the lower amount counts. Moving $AGI to a second wallet to vote twice doesn't work.
    3. 10,000 $AGI to vote (the Crush level). Anyone can see the results.
    4. A few days a vote (3 unless it says). Change your choice as often as you like until it closes.
    5. The result counts. The team follows it unless it's unsafe, illegal or not possible, and says why if it doesn't.

    The fine print

    • Voting is a free signature that proves the wallet is yours. It is never a transaction, costs no gas and gives no access to your tokens. Anyone asking you to send $AGI or approve anything “to vote” is not us.
    • Results show each choice's $AGI and number of wallets, never who voted for what. Your wallet is kept as a scrambled code; its address is kept encrypted only until the final count, then deleted.
    • Votes give the community a say. They aren't shares, rights or a promise of anything. Not financial advice: $AGI is a meme token and can lose all of its value. 18+.
