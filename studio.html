How AGI Studio works, and its rules
  • 18+, PG-13, AI-generated. Everything here is made by AI services and labeled as AI-made. Adults only, nothing sexual or violent, never a child or anyone made to look like one.
  • Real people only with their OK. "From a photo" takes a photo of you, of an adult who said you may use it, or a character of your own: you tick a box saying so first. Never a famous person, never someone who didn't agree, never someone else's character or a brand's mascot. An influencer made from a photo of a real person stays private (never in Explore, never liked or boosted: only people with its link see it), and deleting it deletes its photo and everything made from it.
  • Credits. 1 credit = 1 cent. You buy them with USDG, sent from your own wallet app to the studio's wallet: this page never asks your wallet to send anything, and our server only reads the payment on the chain. Credits have no cash value: they can't be withdrawn, sold or refunded. Something that can't be made gives its credits back.
  • Holder perks. Crush (10,000+ $AGI held or burned for messages): 20% off and 100 free credits a month. Soulmate (1,000,000+): 40% off, 400 free credits a month and a free 3D model once a month. A thank-you for holding, not an investment.
  • Gifts and invites. You can give some of the credits you bought (or got as a gift) to another wallet, by its address or the clank.trade name it shows here, or as a gift link anyone with it can claim once: held until then, you can take it back while nobody has, and after 30 days it comes back by itself. From 10 credits a gift, up to 50,000 a day; this month's free credits and bonus credits can't be given. Your invite link brings a friend who never bought credits 100 bonus credits with their first top-up of $5 or more, and you 10% of what they buy as bonus credits (up to 500 from each friend, 5,000 a month). Bonus credits are spent after your free ones and before bought ones, and never run out. Credits have no cash value: a gift is a gift, never a sale. Never buy credits from anyone: nobody from AGI Studio ever asks you to send credits.
  • Burns. You can burn $AGI from your own wallet app (to the burn address, where nobody can reach it) for a 3D model without credits, for one more influencer, or to boost one of yours in Explore for a day. A burn is gone for good: not an investment, no refunds, not financial advice.
  • Likes and the spotlight of the week. A connected wallet can like other people's photos and videos. Holders' likes pick the spotlight of the week; its maker may get a prize in $AGI, sent to the wallet that made it: a thank-you for making, not an investment.
  • Downloads carry a small mark. Photos and videos you download say "Made with AGI Studio · AI-generated" in a corner, so a post that travels says it's AI.
  • X banners and captions. An X banner is a photo made exactly 1500 × 500 for your X header. Ready-made captions are free and instant; captions written by AI are free too, 30 a day (only your influencer's name and what the post shows go to the AI service). Every caption says it's AI-made with a hashtag and never talks about money or coins, and posting is yours: "Post on X" only opens X's own post box.
  • Your clank.trade name, if you like. It shows as "by @name" on your public influencers and in Explore. Our server reads that public clank.trade profile and keeps the name only when the profile's wallet is the one you connected here (read again about once a week). You can remove it anytime in My studio.
  • A coin with its face. You launch it from your own wallet on the launch page, on clank.trade's own contracts: the coin is yours, and we never promote it. A coin is public and permanent. Meme coins can lose all of their value.
  • Help and support. The helper answers questions about AGI Studio with AI, from our own guide: only your question (and your one or two before it) goes to the AI service, and its answers can be wrong. For anything else, open a ticket in Help & support: a person on the team answers you there. Never write a seed phrase, a private key or a password anywhere: we never ask for one, and we never ask you to send money, sign anything or connect your wallet to another site.
  • The team and bans. The owner and moderators look at reports and can take things down. A wallet that breaks these rules can be banned: it can't make anything here any more (its credits stay, and anything still being made stops and gives its credits back), and its influencers, their pictures and their videos are hidden from everyone. It can still open a support ticket to ask why, and a moderator can lift a ban.
  • What's kept. Your influencers, their pictures and videos, their likes, the clank.trade name you show (if you do), your support tickets (what you write and the team's answers), your gifts (who each was for or from, as a clank.trade name or a short address, and its note) and who invited you, while AGI Studio is private whether your wallet is on its list (its short address and the owner's note), and a scrambled code of your wallet (never its address). Photos and clips you upload are checked by an AI service before they're kept, and a photo is deleted once its influencer is made. Anyone can report anything here; what shouldn't be here comes down.
  • Not financial advice. Nothing here is.
