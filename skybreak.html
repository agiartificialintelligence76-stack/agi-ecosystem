HOW TO PLAY

KEYBOARD & MOUSE

  • W A S DMove
  • SPACEJump. Hold for height; jump at a ledge to climb it
  • SHIFTSprint
  • QHold as you land to roll and keep your speed
  • EHold near a diamond to swing
  • RRecover to your rift or the start
  • MOUSELook around; the wheel zooms
  • ESCPause

TOUCH

  • Put your left thumb down anywhere on the left to move. Push to the edge to sprint
  • Jump. Hold for height; jump at a ledge to climb it
  • Lights up near a diamond: hold to swing
  • Hold as you land to roll and keep your speed
  • Hold a moment to recover to your rift or the start
  • Drag anywhere else to look

READ THE CHART

  • Green candle. Stand on it and it pumps you up; the glowing wick shows how high
  • Red candle. It dumps a moment after you land. Jump first
  • Rug. It gets pulled out from under you. Keep moving
  • Rails and pads. Green rails fling you long; gold pads throw you up
  • Lasers. They sweep the outer edge, so stay inside
  • Moon gravity. The last zone is floaty: bigger gaps, bigger jumps

Gamepad: left stick moves, right stick looks, A jumps, RB swings, LB sprints, B rolls. Falls stay in the world: miss a jump and you land on the loop below. Rift runs save on side paths; Pure ascent never saves. Coins and best times stay on this device.
