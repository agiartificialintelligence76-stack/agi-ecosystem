WASD · Space
Move and jump. Hold Space for height. Jump toward a nearby ledge to mantle up.
Green candle
Stand on it and it pumps. The glowing wick and frame show exactly how high it goes.
Red candle
It dumps a moment after you land. Jump before it drops away.
Rug · rails · pads
Rugs get pulled out from under you. Green rails fling you long. Gold pads throw you up.
Lasers · diamonds
Red lasers sweep the outer edge, so stay inside. Hold E near a diamond to swing.
Moon gravity
The last zone is low gravity: bigger gaps, floatier jumps.
R · Escape
Recover to your rift or the start. Pause the run.
Touch
Left stick to move, drag anywhere to look. ↑ jumps (hold for height), hold E to swing, ↺ recovers, Ⅱ pauses.
Gamepad
Sticks move and look. A jumps, RB swings, LB sprints, B rolls.

Falls stay in the world: miss a jump and you land on the loop below. Rift runs save on side paths; Pure ascent never saves. Coins and best times stay on this device.
